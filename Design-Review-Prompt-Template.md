# Design Review Prompt — Reusable Template

Copy this, fill the **[brackets]**, attach your screen capture, and send.

---

## ▶ Quick prompt (copy-paste)

> **Adam**, review this page design: **[PAGE_URL]**
> Benchmark it against our standard: **[STANDARD_URL]**
> I've attached a screen capture of the page (PDF/PNG).
>
> Do this:
> 1. Audit every design **and** content error, section by section, and **number each issue directly on the captured screenshots**.
> 2. Show **before → after** fixes for the top 3 issues.
> 3. Give me a **PDF**. The **last page** must be a **scorecard out of 10** — a category breakdown (hierarchy, consistency, typography, colour, spacing, content accuracy, brand alignment) rolling up to one overall score.
>
> Deliverable style: **[audit + fixes + after-mockups]**  ·  Output: **[editable Canva + exported PDF]**  ·  Scoring: **[category breakdown + overall /10]**

---

## ▶ Answer these up front (saves a round of questions)

- **Deliverable depth:** audit + fixes + after-mockups  /  audit only  /  full redesign of every section
- **Output:** editable Canva + PDF  /  PDF only
- **Scoring:** category breakdown + overall /10  /  overall /10 only
- **Brand source:** the exact logo/colour to treat as truth (e.g. the live site or a brand-kit URL)

---

## ▶ Always-check list (paste in if you want a thorough pass)

> While reviewing, specifically flag:
> - **Currency / number consistency** (same figure shown two ways, missing thousands separators)
> - **Comparisons that don't reconcile** (mixed currencies, or per-month vs per-year)
> - **Name / spelling / capitalisation** inconsistencies for people and brands
> - **Grid balance** (odd item counts leaving empty cells; column counts that change row to row)
> - **Repetition** (the same CTA banner or quote/photo used over and over)
> - **Stray or misplaced elements** (floating buttons, orphaned icons)
> - **Promo / version mismatches** vs the live standard
> - **Draft artefacts** (admin bar visible, `?page_id=` URLs instead of clean permalinks)
> - **Readability** (line length, body-text contrast)

---

## ▶ Canva note (so the editable step doesn't stall)

Canva only turns a file into an editable design from a **public link** or a **manual upload**. Either:
- drag the delivered PDF into Canva → *Create a design → Import file*, **or**
- put the PDF on a public link (Drive/Dropbox "anyone with link") and paste it to Adam to import automatically.
